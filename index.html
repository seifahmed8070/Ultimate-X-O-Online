How to Play

1. The Ultimate Board: It's a large 3x3 grid, where each cell contains a smaller 3x3 grid.

2. The Catch: You don't get to pick which small board to play in. The move your opponent makes dictates where you must play next.

3. Full/Won Boards: If you are sent to a small board that is already won or full, you unlock the ability to play anywhere on the entire ultimate board!

4. Winning: Win 3 small boards in a row to win the Match!
